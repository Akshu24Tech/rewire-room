# Brief: Froze when my lead asked for ideas in standup

Date: 2026-10-31 · Type: work-meeting · Mode: debrief

## The moment
Daily standup, seven people on a video call, cameras on. Near the end my team lead
asked, "Any ideas on the caching slowdown?" I had a real idea (cache the serializer
output, not just the query). Stakes felt high because I am new and want to be taken
seriously.

## What I wanted
To say my idea clearly, in the moment, while the question was open.

## What I did
I waited. I rehearsed the sentence in my head three or four times to get it perfect.
About twenty seconds later a senior dev said a rougher version of the same idea, the
lead said "good call", and the thread moved on. I stayed on mute for the rest of the
call.

## Discomfort
Before 5/10 · During 8/10 · After 6/10 (mostly frustration at myself)

## Context
Slept ~6 hours. Standup is at 9am, first thing, before coffee. This is the third time
this month I have had the idea and not said it in time. (assumed) I replay these for
hours afterward.

## Next similar moment
Tomorrow's standup, same time, same people.
