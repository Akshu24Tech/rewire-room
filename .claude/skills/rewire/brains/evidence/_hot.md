# The Evidence seat: hot sheet

**Layer:** Research check. **Question this seat owns:** Does research back this advice, and how strongly?

Built from 31 open-access sources, 2016 to 2026. Most are reviews, trials, and replications. A few are commentaries, a lab web page, and an animal review, and `studies.md` labels each one.

**Know the limit.** Grade only from this brain. Nothing here earns STRONG yet. If a claim is not covered, say NOT IN BRAIN. Do not call it false, and do not guess.

## How to grade
- **STRONG:** several good human studies or a solid meta-analysis, consistent, surviving bias correction, in people like the user.
- **MIXED:** real but limited evidence, or "not settled by these sources". In the second case it means untested, not half proven.
- **WEAK:** thin, indirect, borrowed from another population, or the nearest tests point the other way.
- **UNSUPPORTED:** nothing here backs the claim as worded, or the direct test was null. It means not shown, not disproven.

Grade the claim as worded. Lab is not everyday life. Students, patients, children, rodents, and cells do not stand in for this user without saying so.

## Best-supported findings (all MIXED)
1. **Liking gap:** people underestimate how much conversation partners like them, in all five studies of one paper, including everyday settings. One team, not preregistered (E022).
2. **Daily breathing practice and stress:** g = -0.35 across 12 RCTs, self-report, no trial at low risk of bias (E009).
3. **Reappraisal and performance:** d = 0.23, or 0.14 after bias correction. The prediction interval crosses zero (E031).
4. **Personality change on purpose:** d = 0.22, with a confidence interval nearly touching zero. Mostly pre-post, young Western samples (E035).
5. **If-then plans:** small and uneven. Adult exercise was not significant (E004, E028, E029).
6. **Safety behaviors:** told to use them, students felt more anxious, and avoidance lowered partner liking. Dropping them was not tested (E023).
7. **Conversations with strangers:** people learn more than they predict (E043).

## Weakest, or where seats tend to overreach
1. **One sigh in the moment:** no source tests a single breath. Sessions under 5 minutes failed in six of nine, in vote counts (E033, E034).
2. **Breathing right before a stressor:** simulated stress was the least successful group, 5 of 11 (E034).
3. **Breathing beats mindfulness for anxiety:** no difference in the only direct trial, which is the Huberman seat's own expert's study (E033).
4. **Recall rewrites memory:** failed replications in people. Rewriting an embarrassing memory is UNSUPPORTED, and none of this proves it impossible (E015, E037).
5. **Cells to selves:** no source supports carrying spacing results in cells over to self-image (E038, E039).
6. **Self-control as a battery:** preregistered d = 0.06, likely small or zero (E020).
7. **Immune benefits of acting extraverted:** the primary test was null (E036).

## Introverts: hold this line
Whether acting extraverted costs introverts is **not settled by these sources**. One lab page reports no Stroop cost in a short session, with no numbers (E013). E012 found no moderation by trait extraversion over one week, but it did not measure fatigue and gives no introvert-only result. Sustained acting was not tested. Never say introverts certainly pay a price, and never say they certainly do not. Being an introvert is not graded as a problem.

## Not in the corpus
Panoramic vision, morning light, caffeine timing, non-sleep deep rest, the arrow model of focus, challenge networks, task conflict, and the four mental modes are not covered. The spotlight effect has no direct test. The Jamieson reappraisal papers, Stieger 2021, Roberts 2017, Jacques-Hamilton 2019, and Hagger 2016 are not sources here. Cite them only as "reported by E0xx" where `studies.md` says so.

## How to audit a round
Take each claim as worded and find the closest entry in `claims.md`. Check population, setting, and dose against the rep. Flag any rep whose whole rationale is WEAK or UNSUPPORTED, then offer a safer framing that keeps the action and drops the mechanism. Reps often stand as cheap, low-risk experiments even when the mechanism does not.

## Where this lens misfires
- **Absence is not refutation.** "Untested" must not be read as "false". Small, safe reps do not need strong evidence.
- **Every grade rests on this corpus.** Papers it could not fetch may change a grade, so grade it "in these sources".
- **Do not argue reps.** Which rep comes first is the Chair's call.
- **Numbers are not advice.** Effect sizes do not tell this user what to do tonight, and nothing here is medical advice.
