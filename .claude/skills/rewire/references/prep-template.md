# Prep template

Write `prep.md` in exactly this shape. Plain English, short sentences, no em dashes. One tool, one intention, one if-then. Do not stack a routine; the user is about to walk into something, not study.

```markdown
# Prep: <one-line moment>

YYYY-MM-DD · For: <when the moment is> · Seats: <Short names>

## Right before

**<The one state or energy tool, from the state or energy seat.>** <One line on how to do it.> (<seat>, <source id>)

## Walking in

Hold this: **<the one intention, from the sharpest seat>.** (<seat>)

If-then: When <the moment you expect to freeze>, I will <small action>.

## Notice this

<One thing to pay attention to during the moment, so you can debrief it after.>

---
After it happens, run `/rewire debrief` and tell me how it went. That is where it gets logged and turns into your next rep.

Seats are simulations built from public work. Not therapy or medical advice.
```
